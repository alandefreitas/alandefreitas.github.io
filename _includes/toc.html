{% comment %} Table of contents built from the headings on the page. Parameters: `root` (CSS selector of the element whose headings are listed), `levels` (comma-separated heading tags, e.g. "h2,h3") and `inline` (set to false to suppress the inline rendering). Two renderings of the same list: a column floating in the left margin on wide screens that follows the reader and marks the section being read, and, on narrower screens, one line of links under the title. The inline rendering uses the deepest heading levels whose labels fit in about 200 characters (one line of links for a single level, one line per top-level heading when there are two), and is dropped when even the top level does not fit. Nothing is hard-coded; a page opts in by wrapping its content in `.toc-layout` and including this file. {% endcomment %}
